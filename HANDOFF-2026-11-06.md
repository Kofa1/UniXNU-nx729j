Why 29 MiB rather than the previous ~95 MiB: earlier images carried the 93 MiB FAT as a raw Android ramdisk. ENTRY-05 gzip-compresses the FAT together with the firmware. The decompressed bundle remains **101,715,968 bytes**. Kernel and payload files were not removed.

The wrapper solves compatibility without modifying ABL or shared drivers:
1. Bundle layout: 4 KiB board shim + unchanged 3 MiB FD + 1 MiB handoff-DTB slot + unchanged 93 MiB FAT.
2. It chooses a 4 KiB trampoline at `0xA7000000` or `0xB7F00000` outside the incoming source range.
3. Using overlap-safe forward/backward copying, relocates the bundle to `0xA8000000`, within the existing reserved `Kernel` range `0xA8000000..0xB8000000`.
4. Restores original FD to `0xA7100000` and passes x0 = `0xA8301000`, a normal FDT whose chosen/initrd properties point to embedded FAT at `0xA8401000` with its full size.
5. Shared SEC, UniXNURamDiskDxe, DiskIo/FAT, UniXNU and DarwinBoot proceed normally. Android ramdisk size is zero.

### Crypto start-once fix

iPadOS 27 uses `XNU_CRYPTO_BOOTSTRAP=source`. Shared `native-security-init.patch` lets OSKext reuse `source_corecrypto14_start_once` for the original corecrypto that early bootstrap already started. A legacy NX729J `prelinked-corecrypto-oskext.patch` removed that handling, causing duplicate PRNG registration in source-bootstrap mode.

Fix in pushed commit `e6e4e2b`: board selects `patches/xnu/series-source`, which retains shared behavior and excludes the legacy removal patch. Old `patches/xnu/series` remains for older prelinked-bootstrap configurations. Assertions/selftests were not removed and callbacks were not fabricated.

`docs/reference/corecrypto-entry04.md` records this. ENTRY-05 carries the resulting ENTRY-04 kernel; the earlier PRNG assertion is no longer the first phone stop.
